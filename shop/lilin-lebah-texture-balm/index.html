Information

Add shape and texture to your style with our Lilin Lebah Texture Balm. This flexible, non-sticky balm builds volume, gives soft hold and keeps natural movement without stiffness, even through a humid afternoon. Beeswax from Malaysian bee farms, shea butter and candlenut oil keep hair nourished while you style. Good for short or long hair, it is made for messy texture, soft waves and polished looks.

Benefits

Defines, Adds Volume, Holds in Humidity, Tames Flyaways

Ingredients

Cera Alba (Beeswax), Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Aleurites Moluccanus (Candlenut) Seed Oil, Tocopherol (Vitamin E), Cetearyl Alcohol, Hydrolyzed Rice Protein, Caprylyl Glycol, Cymbopogon Citratus Oil.
